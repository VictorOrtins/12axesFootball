### Axis "pos" Possession: Ball (Posse) x Counter (Contra-ataque)
personaBrief (1-2 sentences) for THIS axis, then answer its 5 questions.
1. [id=pos_02] Dominar um jogo é criar mais perigo, mesmo tendo menos a bola.
   EN: Dominating a game means creating more danger, even with less of the ball.
   Agree means: A team dominates when it creates more chances, even if the opponent keeps the ball most of the time.
   Disagree means: Dominating a game means having the ball, and a team that doesn't have it is being dominated.
2. [id=pos_03] Contra um adversário mais fraco que se fecha, meu time deve ficar com a bola e rodá-la até achar o espaço.
   EN: Against a weaker side that sits back, my team should keep the ball and move it around until a gap opens.
   Agree means: Against a team that sits back, your side keeps the ball and moves it patiently until the opponent leaves a gap.
   Disagree means: Against a team that sits back, your side can hand the ball back to draw the opponent out and attack the space it leaves.
3. [id=pos_05] Ter a bola também é uma forma de se defender, porque enquanto ela está com a gente o rival não ataca.
   EN: Having the ball is also a way of defending, because while we have it the opponent can't attack.
   Agree means: Possession also protects the team, and keeping the ball is a way of not being attacked.
   Disagree means: Defending is done without the ball, and keeping it doesn't make the team any safer.
4. [id=pos_01] Quem tem a bola manda no jogo, então o time deve sempre querer mais posse que o adversário.
   EN: Whoever has the ball controls the game, so a team should always want more possession than its opponent.
   Agree means: The team tries to have more of the ball than the opponent in every game, because whoever has possession sets the pace.
   Disagree means: Having more or less of the ball doesn't matter in itself, and the team can give it up without losing control of the game.
5. [id=pos_04] Com atacantes rápidos no elenco, deixo o rival ficar com a bola para sobrar espaço nas costas da defesa dele.
   EN: With fast forwards in the squad, I let the opponent have the ball so there is space behind their defense.
   Agree means: With fast forwards, the team gives up the ball on purpose so the opponent pushes up and leaves space behind.
   Disagree means: Even with fast forwards, the team prefers to have the ball and use their pace some other way.

### Axis "pos" Possession: Ball (Posse) x Counter (Contra-ataque)
personaBrief (1-2 sentences) for THIS axis, then answer its 5 questions.
1. [id=pos_02] Dominar um jogo é criar mais perigo, mesmo tendo menos a bola.
   EN: Dominating a game means creating more danger, even with less of the ball.
   Agree means: A team dominates when it creates more chances, even if the opponent keeps the ball most of the time.
   Disagree means: Dominating a game means having the ball, and a team that doesn't have it is being dominated.
2. [id=pos_05] Ter a bola também é uma forma de se defender, porque enquanto ela está com a gente o rival não ataca.
   EN: Having the ball is also a way of defending, because while we have it the opponent can't attack.
   Agree means: Possession also protects the team, and keeping the ball is a way of not being attacked.
   Disagree means: Defending is done without the ball, and keeping it doesn't make the team any safer.
3. [id=pos_04] Com atacantes rápidos no elenco, deixo o rival ficar com a bola para sobrar espaço nas costas da defesa dele.
   EN: With fast forwards in the squad, I let the opponent have the ball so there is space behind their defense.
   Agree means: With fast forwards, the team gives up the ball on purpose so the opponent pushes up and leaves space behind.
   Disagree means: Even with fast forwards, the team prefers to have the ball and use their pace some other way.
4. [id=pos_03] Contra um adversário mais fraco que se fecha, meu time deve ficar com a bola e rodá-la até achar o espaço.
   EN: Against a weaker side that sits back, my team should keep the ball and move it around until a gap opens.
   Agree means: Against a team that sits back, your side keeps the ball and moves it patiently until the opponent leaves a gap.
   Disagree means: Against a team that sits back, your side can hand the ball back to draw the opponent out and attack the space it leaves.
5. [id=pos_01] Quem tem a bola manda no jogo, então o time deve sempre querer mais posse que o adversário.
   EN: Whoever has the ball controls the game, so a team should always want more possession than its opponent.
   Agree means: The team tries to have more of the ball than the opponent in every game, because whoever has possession sets the pace.
   Disagree means: Having more or less of the ball doesn't matter in itself, and the team can give it up without losing control of the game.

### Axis "pro" Progression: Patient build-up (Construção paciente) x Direct play (Jogo direto)
personaBrief (1-2 sentences) for THIS axis, then answer its 5 questions.
1. [id=pro_01] Se o centroavante está livre nas costas da defesa, a bola longa é o passe certo.
   EN: If the striker is free behind the defense, the long ball is the right pass.
   Agree means: With the striker free behind the defense, the team plays it long without a second thought.
   Disagree means: Even with the striker free, the team prefers to move up with short passes over risking the long ball.
2. [id=pro_04] Prefiro um volante que nunca perde a bola a um que arrisca passes para quebrar linhas.
   EN: I prefer a defensive midfielder who never loses the ball to one who risks line-breaking passes.
   Agree means: The ideal defensive midfielder is safe on the ball and prefers the sure pass to the risky one.
   Disagree means: The ideal defensive midfielder tries passes between the lines, even if it means losing the ball more often.
3. [id=pro_03] Cada passe para o lado ou para trás é uma chance de ataque desperdiçada.
   EN: Every sideways or backward pass is a wasted chance to attack.
   Agree means: The team always looks for the forward pass, and going sideways or back is wasting time.
   Disagree means: Sideways and backward passes are part of the build-up and help find the right moment to go forward.
4. [id=pro_05] Quando o time recupera a bola, tem que chegar à finalização em três ou quatro toques.
   EN: When the team wins the ball back, it has to get a shot away within three or four touches.
   Agree means: After winning the ball back, the team goes for goal as fast as possible, with few touches.
   Disagree means: After winning the ball back, the team can hold it and get organized before attacking.
5. [id=pro_02] Se o rival pressiona alto, a saída tem que ser por baixo, trocando passes, mesmo com risco perto da área.
   EN: If the opponent presses high, we should still play out from the back with short passes, even with the risk near our own box.
   Agree means: Even under pressure, the team plays out with short passes from the goalkeeper and accepts the risk of losing the ball near its box.
   Disagree means: Under a high press, the team kicks it forward or goes long, because losing the ball near your own box costs too much.

### Axis "est" Structure: Positional (Posicional) x Relational (Relacional)
personaBrief (1-2 sentences) for THIS axis, then answer its 5 questions.
1. [id=est_02] Os melhores ataques nascem de jogadores bem próximos, tabelando em pouco espaço como numa roda de bobinho.
   EN: The best attacks come from players close together, combining in tight spaces like in a rondo.
   Agree means: The team attacks with many players near the ball, playing short passes and one-twos in tight spaces.
   Disagree means: The team attacks with players spread across the pitch, each one occupying a space, to stretch the opposing defense.
2. [id=est_01] Cada jogador deve ocupar sua zona do campo, para o time manter a estrutura mesmo atacando.
   EN: Each player should hold his zone of the pitch, so the team keeps its shape even when attacking.
   Agree means: Each player has a set zone and sticks to it, so the team is always well spread across the pitch.
   Disagree means: Players can leave their zones to join teammates wherever the play is.
3. [id=est_04] O centroavante deve sair da área para tabelar com os meias, não só esperar a bola.
   EN: The striker should drop out of the box to combine with the midfielders, not just wait for the ball.
   Agree means: The striker roams, drops deep to play one-twos and helps build the attack.
   Disagree means: The striker stays in the box, where he is most dangerous, and waits for the ball to reach him.
4. [id=est_03] Se o ponta abandona a linha lateral para buscar jogo no meio, ele está errando a função.
   EN: If the winger leaves the touchline to come inside for the ball, he is getting his job wrong.
   Agree means: The winger stays wide, on the touchline, even when the ball is on the other side or in the middle.
   Disagree means: The winger can leave the flank and come inside to join the play.

5. [id=est_05] Um bom time se reconhece por posições bem definidas, em que cada um sabe exatamente onde estar em cada fase do jogo.
   EN: A good team is recognized by well-defined positions, where everyone knows exactly where to be in each phase of play.
   Agree means: A good team has clear, rehearsed positions, and each player knows where to be with and without the ball.
   Disagree means: A good team organizes itself during the game, with players deciding together where to stand.

### Axis "lar" Width: Through the middle (Por dentro) x Down the wings (Pelas pontas)
personaBrief (1-2 sentences) for THIS axis, then answer its 5 questions.
1. [id=lar_03] Contra uma defesa fechada no meio, o melhor é abrir o campo e levar a bola para a área pelos lados.
   EN: Against a defense packed in the middle, the best option is to stretch the pitch and get the ball into the box from the flanks.
   Agree means: Against a crowded middle, the team goes wide and gets into the box from the flanks, with crosses and cut-backs.
   Disagree means: Even against a crowded middle, the team keeps trying through the center, with one-twos and short passes to break the block.
2. [id=lar_01] Jogos se ganham com pontas vencendo o lateral no um contra um e cruzando.
   EN: Games are won by wingers beating the full-back one-on-one and crossing.
   Agree means: The route to goal goes down the flanks, with the winger beating the full-back and crossing into the box.
   Disagree means: The route to goal goes through the middle, and crosses are just one option among others.
3. [id=lar_04] Prefiro escalar um meia a mais por dentro a manter dois pontas abertos colados na linha.
   EN: I'd rather pick an extra central midfielder than keep two wingers hugging the touchline.

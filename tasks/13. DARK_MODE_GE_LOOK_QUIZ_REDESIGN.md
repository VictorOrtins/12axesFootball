# Dark mode, GE-style look and quiz redesign

Status: A done (2026-11-05), not committed yet. B and C not started. Branch `football-logic`.

**Decided (user, 2026-11-05):** dark is the default when nothing is saved; the PDF follows the chosen theme; the quiz keeps the five answer levels and only changes the look, and keeps the axis label, the (?) help and the question count. Still open: which GE parts to take, and the fonts (B1).

The user asked for three things:
- dark mode for the whole site, not only the quiz
- a look closer to the GE site (https://ge.globo.com/)
- a quiz screen closer to the Anti-Personality test (https://antipersonality.webflow.io/)

Suggested order: A, then B, then C. A is mostly plumbing and makes B cheaper, since every new colour in B then gets its dark value at the same time. C comes last because it sits on the palette and type that B sets.

## Where we are today

- Colours all go through tokens. `app.css`, `editorial.css`, `results.css`, `motion.css` and `report.css` have no hex or rgba values; only `tokens.css` and `theme-dark.css` do.
- Dark mode exists only for the quiz. `useQuizTheme(active)` (`frontend/src/hooks/useQuizTheme.ts`) sets `html[data-quiz-theme="dark"]` only while `screen` is `quiz` or `archetype` (`App.tsx:116`). `theme-dark.css` overrides the tokens plus about 26 rules with fixed colours.
- The toggle (`QuizThemeToggle`) is shown only in the quiz header (`App.tsx:676`). The default is light, and the choice is saved under `12axes:quiz-theme`.
- Screens: `home`, `variant`, `quiz`, `archetype`, `results`, `catalog` (plus the catalog profile view), and the PDF report.
- Fonts: Sora (display) and Poppins (body).
- Current look: a dark green top bar, a green pitch hero, and pale green-grey rounded cards on a pale background. The home sections fade in on scroll (some cards looked half-faded in a screenshot).
- Current quiz: a card with an axis tag and a (?) button, the statement, and five filled rows with ✓/–/✕ icons and a radio dot. Below them are Back, an "Advance on answer" switch and Next, with a segmented progress bar at the top.

## B. A look closer to GE

What GE does, from a look on 2026-11-05:
- A solid bright green bar (`#06AA48`-ish), with menu on the left, logo in the centre, and search and login on the right.
- A pale grey page (`#F9F9F9`) with no big rounded panels. Content sits straight on the page, split by thin grey rules.
- Image-led: a big photo hero with the headline in bold white on top of the photo (the main story in upper case) over a dark gradient, and smaller photo tiles beside it.
- Feed items: photo on the left, then a small "chapéu" label (the section name, semibold), a large bold green headline, a related link with a bullet, then grey metadata ("Há 46 minutos — Em fórmula 1").
- A right column with a compact table (league table with crests and form dots).
- Horizontal rows of tall rounded video cards with a duration badge.
- Open Sans, bold, tight leading on headlines. Dense, newspaper-like, square corners on most things.

This is a look to borrow, not a copy: do not use GE's or Globo's name, logo, fonts (Globotipo) or exact layout. The app must not look like it is a Globo product.

Tasks:
- B1. **Decide (user):** which parts matter most? Suggested set: bright green bar, flat pale page with thin rules, image-led cards with chapéu labels and big bold headlines, and denser spacing. Also decide whether to keep Sora/Poppins or switch to an Open Sans-like font (Open Sans is free on Google Fonts / Fontsource).
- B2. New palette in `tokens.css`: brighter green for the bar and headline links, grey page, a near-black text colour, and grey for metadata. Each new token gets its dark value in the same change (task A).
- B3. Top bar: a solid bright green bar, with the logo centred or left, the nav and the theme toggle. It must still work on a phone (menu button).
- B4. Home: replace the pitch hero with an image-led hero (big bold headline on a dark gradient). Turn the "how it works", "12 axes" and "versions" sections into flat sections split by rules instead of rounded panels. Check the scroll fade-in: in the screenshot some cards were still half faded.
- B5. Catalog and profile pages: these fit GE best. Use a feed or grid of cards with the crest or photo, a chapéu (team, manager, ideology, or the era), a bold headline name, and grey metadata (period, nearest match).
- B6. Results: chapéu labels on sections, bold headlines, a compact table for the nearest teams and managers (like GE's league table: crest, name, score).
- B7. Corners: smaller radius across the site (GE is mostly square). Done as a token change.
- B8. Check the photo licences still show where needed (task 12, 5.4) after the cards change.

Done when: the home, catalog, results and variant screens use the new bar, palette and card style in both themes, at desktop and phone width.

## C. Quiz screen like the Anti-Personality test

What that quiz does, from a look on 2026-11-05:
- One question per screen, centred. The question is short, in upper case and bold, with a lot of empty space around it.
- Answers are full-width outline buttons (thin 1px border, no fill, no icons), stacked with even gaps and the text centred.
- On hover the button inverts: a solid fill with the text in the background colour.
- Clicking an answer moves straight to the next question. There are no Back or Next buttons on screen.
- Progress is one thin bar under the answers, outlined, with a single accent-colour fill. There is no "Question 1 of 36" text.
- A monospaced/compressed font, black background and white text. Nothing else is on the screen.

Tasks:
- C1. **Decide (user):** how far to go. Suggested: keep our five-point scale and the statements, but take the bare layout, outline buttons, hover invert, and single thin progress bar. Keep the site's fonts unless B changes them.
- C2. Remove the card around the question. Show the axis tag and the (?) help small and quiet, or **decide (user)** whether to hide them during the quiz.
- C3. Answer buttons: outline, centred text, no ✓/–/✕ icons and no radio dot. Hover and keyboard focus invert the button. The chosen answer stays filled when the user comes back to a question.
- C4. Advance on answer always on. Remove the switch. Keep a small, quiet Back link (people misclick), plus the keyboard shortcuts if there are any (check `QuestionCard.tsx`).
- C5. Progress: one thin outlined bar with a single fill, below the answers. **Decide (user):** keep a small "12 / 36" count or drop it.
- C6. A short transition between questions (fade or slide), off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- C7. Both themes: in dark it should look close to the reference (black page, white outlines). In light it is the same layout inverted.
- C8. Check that the archetype stage, saved progress (resume card) and restart still work, and that the buttons are at least 44px tall on a phone.

Done when: the quiz looks and behaves as above in both themes and on a phone, and the frontend tests for question selection and saved progress still pass.

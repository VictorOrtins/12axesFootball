## Phase 2: investigate the three profiles the user doubts

These are analysis first. Any re-research or re-audit is decided only after the findings are shown to the user. All distances below are RMS over the 12 axes, on the vectors in the `*-profiles.json` files (leftPercent, 100 = left pole). The app's `ProfileMatchScorer` uses a different formula but gives the same nearest ideology for Ferguson.

### 2.1 Why does Ferguson land on "Futebol científico soviético"?
Ferguson's nearest ideologies: soviet-scientific-football 19.3, modern-man-to-man 19.8, bielsismo 23.8, total-football 25.1. Even the nearest one is far, so Ferguson matches no ideology well.

What he shares with the Soviet profile:
- leans counter-attack (pos 35 vs 25)
- high block (blo 70 vs 74)
- physical players (jog 33 vs 28)
- youth (bas 78 vs 65)
- authority (lid 74 vs 89)

Where they differ most:
- width: Ferguson goes down the wings (lar 20), the Soviet profile plays inside (61)
- who decides: Ferguson is talent-led (sis 35), the Soviet profile is system-led (75)
- purpose: res 46 vs 25

Likely causes, to check:
1. **Thin evidence.** His research confidence is medium, and the file says "the tactical analysis is undocumented". It has only five dated facts, mostly about wingers and the squad. The audit filled most axes from general knowledge.
2. **Long period.** The profile covers St Mirren, Aberdeen and United from 1974 to 2013, so it averages very different teams.
3. **No fitting ideology.** No ideology in the catalog describes British, wing-based, talent-led, authoritarian football.

Options: re-research Ferguson with a tactical focus (United 1993–2013), or narrow his period. Then re-audit and compare.

**Decided (2026-11-04):** re-research Ferguson. The user found many articles about his style and they may be used. Get the links from the user and pass them to the `football-researcher` agent as sources to mine. Then re-audit, replace his vector, and compare his nearest ideologies before and after.

The profile mixes Corinthians 2010–13, Corinthians 2015–16 and Brazil 2016–22 (`profilePeriod` 2010-12-01 to 2022-12-09). The research file says Corinthians 2011–13 and Brazil 2020–22 are thin, and the spell dates come from memory.

**Do this after phase 3.3 (the two Brazil teams).** Splitting Brazil 2016–18 from 2019–22 will show which Tite the user means, and whether his manager profile should be narrowed or kept and accepted as "generalist".

### 2.3 Grêmio 2017 should be closer to Renato Gaúcho
Grêmio 2017 to Renato is 18.1. Renato is only the 6th nearest manager; Del Bosque is 1st at 14.4. Seen from Renato, Grêmio 2017 is his 2nd nearest team, after Santos 1962–63 at 18.0.

Biggest axis gaps (Grêmio vs Renato):
- width: lar 75 (inside) vs 28 (wings). This gap alone explains most of the distance.
- possession: pos 59 vs 40
- game plan: pla 61 (own identity) vs 34 (adaptation)
- structure: est 38 vs 24

Cause: Renato's profile counts five spells from 2007 to 2025 (Fluminense twice, Grêmio 2016–21, Flamengo and others). The 2017 Grêmio team played inside, with Luan and Arthur, and the rest of his career pulls his average toward the wings.

Options:
- (a) narrow Renato's counted spells to Grêmio 2016–21, then re-audit;
- (b) keep Renato as a whole-career profile and accept the gap;
- (c) recheck Grêmio 2017's width answers against its research.

**Decision for the user.**

## Workflow reminders
- New profile: `/new-manager` or `/new-team` (launches `football-researcher`; the main session must not research), then `python3 profile-audit/audit_prompt.py <catalog> <id>`, then the `football-auditor` agent given only the prompt path, then `python3 profile-audit/validate.py audit <catalog> <id>`.
- Agents must name scratch scripts after the id.
- Background removal: `~/.venvs/rembg/bin/rembg i -m u2net_human_seg <in> <out>`. Wikimedia: wait 3 s between requests.
- Run the app: backend `cd backend && sh mvnw -o compile exec:java`, frontend `cd frontend && npm run dev`.
- VibeWise learning mode is paused; do not switch it on.

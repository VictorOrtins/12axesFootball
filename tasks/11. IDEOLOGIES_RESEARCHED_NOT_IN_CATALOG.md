## Data quality notes (all in each file's `openQuestions`)

- Set pieces have no verified fact for most of the 14; marking is unverified or disputed for several (Leicester, Inter, Capello, Scolari, Deschamps, Spain, Zidane, Scaloni, Napoli).
- Many spell dates come from search summaries or memory: Mourinho at Inter, Ranieri at Leicester, Gasperini's exit (about 2025-06), Trapattoni's Juventus dates, De Zerbi's Sassuolo, Brighton and Marseille dates, Sarri's Napoli dates, Arsenal cut-off 2022-05-22, Capello's Milan start (month only).
- `validate.py` rejects year ranges in `eventDate`, so whole-spell facts carry a representative season (for example `1980/81` in zona-mista). Same limit as Barcelona 1990–94 in task 10.
- Several quotes are only from search summaries and are not in `statedView` or are marked unverified (Klopp "vertical passes", Deschamps 2025 quote, Marca quote in possession-pragmatism).
- Hamilton's primary texts on relationism ("What is Relationism?", "The Mutants") were not read (403).
- Heavy-metal and mourinhismo mix two different styles each; the open question is recorded in both files.

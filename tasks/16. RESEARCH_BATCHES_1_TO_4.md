# Redo the thin pre-agent profiles (batches 1 to 4)

Status: done 2026-11-06 for the research, the audits and the catalog vectors. Not committed. Branch `research-batch-1` (on top of `hub-fix`).

## Why

The first testers kept getting odd results (see task 14, "Hubs after the first testers"). 22 profiles had been researched before the `football-researcher` agent existed (commit `8297f2b`, 2026-11-02), with few or no tactical sources: Wenger, Miguel Cardoso and Pedro Emanuel had none, and 12 more covered 1 to 8 of the 13 dimensions with few tier A/B pages. Their vectors rested on the auditor's general knowledge. Each was redone with the current pipeline: `football-researcher`, then `football-auditor`, then the vector written into the catalog.

## File conventions

- Old research is kept as `research/<catalog>/<id>.v1.json`.
- `answers/<catalog>/<id>.json` holds the final audit. `.v1.json` is the audit made before the research was redone. For the four profiles that were audited twice (below), `.v2.json` is the first post-research audit.
- `subagent-out/` and `prompts/` hold the raw outputs and prompts of these runs.

## Decisions made without the user (2026-11-06)

The user asked Claude to resolve the open items alone. What was decided:

1. **Two audits, mean vector, for four profiles.** Allegri, Juventus 2014-19, Fluminense 2023 and Flamengo 1980 were each audited twice, and the catalog holds the **mean of the two vectors**, not a single audit. Reasons:
   - Allegri and Juventus: the first Juventus note said the stars had "licence in attack" while the answers read as Sistema on the `sis` axis. A re-audit with a neutral polarity reminder (no target value) gave `sis` 65 for Allegri (was 75) and 70 for Juventus (was 65). The earlier contradiction was in the note, not the answers: the quiz questions ask about being freed from a tactical function, and Allegri demands one from everyone.
   - Fluminense: the research was extended (see 2), so it was re-audited.
   - Flamengo: the fact-check changed the file, so it was re-audited. The two audits then disagreed on `lar` by 39 points (75 and 36) while reading the same evidence, so the mean (about 55) is the better estimate.
   - Re-test noise on the other axes is small: 3 to 8 points on average, 10 at most (Juventus, Fluminense).
2. **Fluminense 2023: marking is a declared gap.** A targeted research run found in-period pressing evidence (Diniz in the Carioca, 2023-02-06: "Marcamos alto ainda... a tendência é marcar alto"), which settles the high-pressing reading. It found nothing naming a zonal or man system for marking, only Diniz after the Club World Cup final: "conseguimos encaixar bem a marcação". `mar` was therefore added to `gaps.axes`, so the audit answers it from general knowledge, as it does for Meisl. The validator notes that the axis has some verified fact; that is intended.
3. **Flamengo 1980: Coutinho stays as the manager of the whole period.** A fact-check could not date his exit (pt.wikipedia says the club president sacked him after a leaked list of released players), found no coach for August to November 1980, and could not settle the Carioca placing (2nd or 3rd). The only evidence supports Coutinho through the Carioca, and nothing contradicts it. `profilePeriod.end` (1980-11-30) is the tournament end, not a Flamengo match.
4. **Milan 1988-91 keeps its window** (two European Cups, 1988-89 and 1989-90) and leaves out the 1987/88 scudetto season. The previous researcher chose it and the user never objected.
5. **Meisl: `blo` and `mar` stay empty in the research.** No source covers Austria's pressing, block or marking, and the audit marks both as general knowledge (values 51 and 60).
6. **Ajax 1994-95 was redone** after Van Gaal's new vector (man-marking at Ajax, `mar` 80 to 25) disagreed with the Ajax team vector (marking 72). The new research agrees (Blind as libero, Reiziger and F. de Boer as man-markers), and `mar` is 25 for both.

## Still open

- **Flamengo 1980:** the date Coutinho left, who coached from August to November 1980, and the Carioca placing. Needs Jornal do Brasil or Jornal dos Sports for December 1980 (`memoria.bn.gov.br`) or FlaEstatística, which cannot be searched from the pipeline.
- **Hugo Meisl:** all evidence is retrospective; no contemporary source and none of Meisl's own writing. Suggested: a Franta/Weisgram biography, Willy Meisl's *Soccer Revolution*, the ANNO newspaper archive.
- **Sources not flagged `retrospectiveOnly`:** Coutinho (7 of 56 flagged), Milan 1988-91 (0 of 50) and Sacchi (0 of 86) are mostly retrospective, but the researchers did not flag them. The auditors may have trusted them as period evidence.
- **Ancelotti:** only two spell dates were read in a source; the rest come from the agent's memory and are flagged in the file. 51% of his facts come from Real Madrid 2021-25, so the vector describes late Ancelotti.
- **Van Gaal:** 64 Ajax facts against 16 for Barcelona; the vector describes Ajax.
- **Simeone:** Estudiantes and River have 3 facts in total.
- **Digest encoding:** `miner.py` writes curly quotes as junk (`ai???...`); verbatim quotes still match once punctuation is ignored.
- **Marking is the weakest axis of all four batches.** It rests on inference for most profiles: Shankly, Renato, Pedro Emanuel, Cardoso, Allegri, Meisl and Fluminense.
- **Quiz hub problem:** research does not fix it (see below); the scorer's hub penalty weight is the next lever.

**Process note (the crashes):** running `rembg` from Python without naming a model uses `bria-rmbg`, which needs 8 to 9 GB of RAM and made the kernel OOM killer take down VS Code twice. Use `new_session("u2net_human_seg")` (about 0.9 GB), cap it with `systemd-run --user --scope -p MemoryMax=3G -p MemorySwapMax=0`, and mine at most 4 URLs per `miner.py` call (an 11-URL call was also killed).
